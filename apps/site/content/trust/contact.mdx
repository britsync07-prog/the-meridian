# Contact

The Meridian is an automatically-curated daily news portal. There is no human editor to contact.

## For Source Publishers

If you are a publisher whose feed is registered with The Meridian and you have questions about your feed's inclusion, coverage, or terms of use verification:

**Email:** sources@meridian.workers.dev

Include your outlet name and feed URL in your message.

## For Readers

### Flag a Source or Report an Error
From any story page, use the "Flag source" or "Report error" button. These submissions enter a review queue that produces a dated correction note on the Corrections page.

### General Inquiries
**Email:** contact@meridian.workers.dev

## Data Requests

For data access, correction, or deletion requests under applicable privacy laws:

**Email:** privacy@meridian.workers.dev

See the [Privacy Policy](/trust/privacy/) for details.

## No Editorial Correspondence

We do not accept:
- Press releases or story pitches
- Requests for coverage or placement
- Editorial feedback on story selection (selection is algorithmic)
- Requests to remove a source from the registry (contact us if terms of use have changed)

All inbound messages are logged. Publisher terms-of-use inquiries are prioritized.
