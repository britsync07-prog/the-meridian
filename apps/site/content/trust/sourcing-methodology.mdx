# Sourcing and Methodology

This page explains how The Meridian selects sources, fetches feeds, normalizes content, clusters stories, and ranks them.

## Source Registry

- **30+ registered publishers** across 7 countries plus Hindi publishers for India.
- **Terms of use verified** before first fetch. Verification date recorded in the registry.
- **Per-outlet metadata**: section priors, ETag/last-modified, consecutive failure count, disabled-until timestamp, freshness SLA.
- **Polite fetching**: Descriptive User-Agent with contact URL, max 20 requests/host/hour, honors `Retry-After` on 429.

## Feed Fetching

- **Scheduled Workers**: 3 cron triggers per day.
- **Conditional GET**: Uses ETag and Last-Modified to avoid redundant transfers.
- **Raw capture only**: The fetch Worker performs no parsing, normalization, or scoring. Raw bytes go to R2 under `raw/{date}/{source}.xml`.
- **Quarantine path**: Non-XML bodies, oversized responses (>2 MB), and bot-protection pages are quarantined per-feed; other feeds continue unaffected.

## Normalization

- **Excerpt budget (hard, tested)**: Max 50 words, 320 characters, 2 sentences, 30% of source visible text. Truncated at word boundary with visible ellipsis. Verbatim only — no paraphrasing, synonymizing, or translation. Source under 160 words publishes no excerpt. Build fails on any violation.
- **Format support**: RSS 2.0, Atom, RDF including `dc:creator`, `media:content`, `content:encoded`.
- **Canonicalization**: Strips `www`, forces HTTPS, normalizes trailing slashes, sorts query params, removes tracking params (`utm_*`, `ref`, `fbclid`, etc.), strips hash fragments.

## Clustering (Merge Rule)

- **Two-gate merge**: Surface similarity + semantic agreement with five anti-merge guards:
  1. Generic-token stop list
  2. Aspect-marker conflict sets
  3. Round/ordinal guard
  4. Distinct-event guard
  5. Entity disjointness (never merge if recognized entity sets are disjoint)
- **Calibrated against fixture**: 200+ hand-labelled headline pairs with measured precision (≥98%) and recall (≥85%). Re-labelled monthly.
- **Reversible merges**: Per-item membership stored; split publishes dated correction note on the story URL.

## Ranking

- **Trending score**: `3.0*log2(1+distinct_outlets) + 1.0*log2(1+mentions) + 5.0*recency_decay(18h) + 0.8*min(1, distinct_sections-1)`
- **Most Covered**: Sorted by coverage count.
- **Editor's Picks**: Top-ranked stories with ≥2 distinct outlets and editorial rank ≤3.

## Section Assignment

Four-stage resolver:
1. Feed URL hint
2. Weighted section lexicon
3. Section overrides
4. Model tie-break (then `world` fallback)

Method and confidence recorded per decision.
