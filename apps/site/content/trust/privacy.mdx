# Privacy Policy

The Meridian collects minimal data. This policy describes what we collect, why, and your rights.

## What We Collect

### Automatic Collection
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** — Aggregated, privacy-first analytics (no cookies, no personal identifiers). Metrics: page views, unique visitors, referrers, country.
- **Server logs** — Standard access logs (IP, user agent, path, timestamp) retained for 30 days for security and debugging.

### No Collection
- No cookies
- No localStorage/sessionStorage
- No tracking pixels
- No third-party analytics (Google Analytics, etc.)
- No user accounts or authentication
- No newsletter signup data stored by us (handled by Brevo, see below)

## Newsletter (Optional)

If you subscribe to a daily edition email:
- **Email address** → Brevo (our email provider)
- **Edition preference** → Brevo
- **Subscription timestamp** → Brevo
- We do not store this data ourselves. See Brevo's privacy policy.

## Data Retention

| Data | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| Cloudflare Web Analytics | 13 months (Cloudflare default) |
| Server access logs | 30 days |
| Newsletter data (Brevo) | Per Brevo policy / your request |

## Your Rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and similar laws you have the right to:
- Access your data
- Rectify inaccurate data
- Erase your data
- Restrict processing
- Data portability
- Object to processing

**To exercise these rights:** Email privacy@meridian.workers.dev with "Data Request" in the subject line.

## Third Parties

| Provider | Purpose | Data Shared |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Cloudflare | Hosting, Workers, R2, D1, Analytics | Access logs, analytics events |
| Brevo | Newsletter delivery (opt-in only) | Email, edition preference |
| Open-Meteo | Weather data (edition centroids) | None (server-side only) |

No data is sold. No advertising networks. No cross-site tracking.

## Security

- All traffic over HTTPS (HSTS, TLS 1.2+)
- Content Security Policy enforced
- `X-Frame-Options: DENY`
- `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`
- `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`
