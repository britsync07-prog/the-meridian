# Editorial Policy

The Meridian operates without a human editor. This policy describes the rules the automation follows.

## Selection Rules

- **Coverage breadth drives prominence.** The lead story is the event with the most distinct outlets reporting on it. Editor's Picks require at least two distinct outlets.
- **No outlet preference.** The ranking algorithm weights distinct outlets heavily. One outlet mentioned five times does not outrank five outlets mentioned once.
- **No topic bias.** The section resolver uses feed URL hints, weighted lexicons, section overrides, and a model tie-break — never editorial judgment about importance.
- **Recency decay is fixed.** The trending score uses an 18-hour half-life. No story is boosted or suppressed based on its age beyond this formula.

## Attribution Rules

- **Every outlet is named.** A story covered by five outlets lists all five on its permanent page. The outlet list is permanent — even if the raw feed item is pruned after 30 days, the outlet attribution remains.
- **Publisher links are followed.** Attribution links carry `rel="noopener"` and never `nofollow` or `sponsored`. They point to the publisher's original article.
- **Excerpts are verbatim.** Every excerpt is a direct quote from the source article, truncated at a word boundary with a visible ellipsis. No paraphrasing, synonymizing, or translation.

## Correction Policy

- **Merges are reversible.** If two items were wrongly merged, a split publishes a dated correction note on the affected story URL.
- **Corrections are append-only.** The Corrections page maintains a live append-only log. No correction is ever deleted or edited after publication.
- **Source errors are flagged.** Readers can flag a source or report a factual error from any story page. Flags enter a queue that produces a dated correction note.

## What We Do Not Do

- We do not write original reporting.
- We do not translate articles (the Hindi edition uses native Hindi publishers; the deterministic template composes from native headlines).
- We do not fact-check source articles.
- We do not express editorial opinions.
- We do not accept payments for placement.
