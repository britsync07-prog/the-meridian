<objective>
Enforce the hard legal excerpt budget across all normalized feed items: clean raw candidate HTML, integrate unicode-compliant word and sentence boundary segmentation (`Intl.Segmenter`) for English and Hindi Devanagari, and connect the normalizer to `shared/contract/excerpt-budget.ts`.

Purpose: The excerpt is the single point where the publication reproduces a publisher's original content. Google explicitly classifies synonymizing, paraphrasing, or translating as scaled-content abuse. The excerpt contract must strictly truncate verbatim, omit excerpts for articles under 160 words, and enforce word/character/sentence/share ceilings across both English and Hindi punctuation (`।`) (CON-01 to CON-05, D-11 to D-15).

Output: Unicode-aware segmentation utilities, HTML text sanitizer, integrated excerpt generation pipeline, and unit tests validating every budget ceiling.
</objective>
