<objective>
Deliver the foundational SimHash-64 fingerprinting engine with 4x16-bit LSH band extraction, generic breaking-news noise filtering, and strict anti-merge guards (CUR-01, CUR-04, CUR-05, CUR-15).

Purpose: Fast candidate generation and defensive clustering require candidate pruning and conservative guards before semantic embeddings are evaluated. Stories must never merge across disjoint entities, across rounds/ordinals (e.g. "Day 1" vs "Day 2"), across conflicting temporal aspects (e.g. "morning" vs "evening"), or across more than 24 hours ("When in doubt, do not merge").

Output: `config/stoplist.ts`, `src/curation/simhash.ts`, `src/curation/guards.ts`, and comprehensive test suites in `tests/curation/simhash.test.ts` and `tests/curation/guards.test.ts`.
</objective>
